Save Vimeo media with a focused workflow for content you are authorized to download.

Vimeo Downloader adds a download control directly to supported Vimeo pages, so you can work from the page you are already watching instead of copying links into another service. When the source provides them, choose the video quality, save an audio stream separately, or download the cover image.

Advantages:

- Choose any video quality offered by the source.
- Download video, audio, or the cover image as separate outputs.
- Trim a DASH or HLS time range before downloading with Premium.
- Queue several downloads and keep browsing while they run.
- Handle large files with automatic splitting or one-file streaming modes.
- See queue progress, completion, failures, and download history in the popup.

The extension works on supported vimeo.com and player.vimeo.com pages. It reads media metadata from the page and does not bypass sign-in, paywalls, DRM, or other access controls. Download only content you have the right to save.

Common uses include saving an authorized Vimeo course lesson, portfolio review, product demo, interview, or reference clip for offline playback or editing. The page-based workflow keeps video, audio, and cover-image downloads together, while the available format and quality follow what the source provides.

How it works: open a supported Vimeo page, choose the video, audio, or thumbnail option, select the available quality, and add the task to the queue. The popup keeps the status visible while you continue browsing. For supported DASH and HLS media, Premium can download only the time range you need.
