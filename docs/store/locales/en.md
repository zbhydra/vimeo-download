Download Vimeo videos as MP4, save the audio as MP3 or M4A, and keep subtitles as VTT files, right from the page you are watching. Free to start.

Vimeo Downloader adds a download panel to supported Vimeo pages. There are no links to copy into another site, because the extension works in your own browser.

What you can save:

- Video: any quality the source offers, up to 4K, as MP4.
- Audio: just the sound, as M4A or MP3.
- Subtitles and captions: each available language as a VTT file, including auto-generated tracks. A VTT file opens in any text editor and works as a timed transcript.
- Cover image: the video's thumbnail.
- A clip: trim a time range from a DASH or HLS quality before downloading.

Built for long videos:

- Queue several downloads and keep browsing while they run.
- Handle large files with automatic splitting or one-file streaming modes.
- See progress, completed files, failures, and download history in the popup.

The extension works on supported vimeo.com and player.vimeo.com pages. It reads media metadata from the page and does not bypass sign-in, paywalls, DRM, or other access controls. Download only content you have the right to save. Vimeo Downloader is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Vimeo, Inc.

Common uses include saving an authorized course lesson, portfolio review, product demo, interview, or reference clip for offline playback or editing.

How it works: open a supported Vimeo page, choose video, audio, subtitles, or image in the panel or popup, then pick the quality or language. The task joins the queue, and the popup shows its status while you keep browsing.
