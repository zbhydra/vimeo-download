# Chrome Web Store Localized Listings

These are the store-facing name, short description, and detailed description for every locale shipped by the extension.

## English (en)

**Name:** Vimeo Downloader - Download HD Video & Audio  
**Short description:** Download Vimeo videos, audio and cover images from vimeo.com and player.vimeo.com in any available quality.

**Detailed description:** Download videos, audio tracks, and cover images from Vimeo pages you are authorized to save. Choose the available video quality, save audio when an audio stream is available, or download the thumbnail separately. Queue downloads and choose how large files are handled. The extension works on supported Vimeo pages and does not bypass access controls.
